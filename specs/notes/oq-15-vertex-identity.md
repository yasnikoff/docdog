# OQ-15: Vertex identity scheme (_key format)

**Status:** RESOLVED (EJ-013)

Auto-generated opaque keys. Meaningful IDs (`DD-ARCH-09`) are indexed fields, not
keys. Content hash as a separate field for change detection. No functional load on
primary keys.
