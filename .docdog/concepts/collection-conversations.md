# Collection: conversations

Raw or lightly-structured chat transcripts worth keeping as working context.

**When to use:** When a session produced valuable reasoning that isn't yet formalized into a discussion, decision, or proposal.

**When not to use:** For completed design reasoning — promote to discussions.
