# Relation: derived_from

A task or record was spawned from an earlier spec, discussion, or observation.

**When to use:** When a task implements a proposal, or an observation motivates a decision. Captures provenance without implying dependency ordering.

**Inverse label:** produced

**Symmetric:** no
