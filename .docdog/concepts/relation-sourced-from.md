# Relation: sourced_from

A was derived from or based on B.

**When to use:** When giving provenance to content that reuses, adapts, or was prompted by another record.

**When not to use:** Not for replacement lineage (supersedes) or composition (part_of). Finer provenance types (derived_from, extends) are deliberately not shipped — register them as project concepts if the distinction matters.

**Inverse label:** source of

**Symmetric:** no
