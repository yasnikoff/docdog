# Collection: observations

Empirical observations from using the project's own tooling — value, friction, retrospectives, dogfooding notes distinct from bug reports (issues) and design reasoning (discussions).

**When to use:** After using a feature or workflow on real work and noticing something worth capturing about how it actually performs — or for periodic assessments of a tool's usefulness in practice.

**When not to use:** For bugs or actionable friction (use issues). For design reasoning or decisions (use discussions or decisions).
