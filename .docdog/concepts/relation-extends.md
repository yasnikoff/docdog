# Relation: extends

A extends B — A builds on B's scope, adding new requirements or capabilities without replacing it.

**When to use:** When a proposal or decision augments a prior one that remains in force. Unlike supersedes, the target stays active.

**Inverse label:** extended by

**Symmetric:** no
