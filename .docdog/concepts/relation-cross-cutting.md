# Relation: cross_cutting

A cross-cuts B — A's concern spans B along with unrelated other contexts.

**When to use:** When a single record (RBAC, logging, observability) applies across many otherwise-disjoint records.

**Inverse label:** cross-cut by

**Symmetric:** no
