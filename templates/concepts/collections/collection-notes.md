# Collection: notes

Miscellaneous context, session notes, and working material that doesn't fit another collection.

**When to use:** When capturing something worth keeping that's not yet a decision, proposal, or discussion record.

**When not to use:** When the content is clearly a decision, proposal, discussion, or issue — use the right collection.
